### Corrections to the benchmark entry

Entry `20261102T010338.038432Z-glimmer-di-decision-index-0-3-and-jevbench-for-the-no-train-c93a40.md` remains immutable. Its scores stand, with these corrections/qualifications from independent review:

This is the original segment's six-decimal normalization correction; the kit scores the recorded choice. The aggregate score has not been changed or silently recomputed from the rounded argmax.
- JevBench cyclic averaging used **two to six option orders per item**, totaling 849 reads, not a uniform five-rotation setup. Both that arm and the raw arm sent each rendered prompt once. No prompt-repetition or gated-thinking arm was run for Glimmer. Cyclic averaging is a different intervention, so its lower score does not reject prompt repetition.
- More context alone cannot recover the wide-option benchmarks. The 313 over-length rows need a larger context; questions over 26 options need an expanded readout codebook. These are different limitations. The reported unsupported counts include the kit's excluded rows where stated, so all-row and scoreable totals differ.
- The answer-mass floor was enforced in the corrected segment, not retrospectively demonstrated for every retained original choice row. The original segment's per-question raw offered-option masses were not retained. Keep that distinction when reproducing the experiment.
