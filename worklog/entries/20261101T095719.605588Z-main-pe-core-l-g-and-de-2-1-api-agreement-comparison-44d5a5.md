# PE-Core L G and DE-2.1 API agreement comparison over 440

## Summary

First speed/agreement comparison of the classification candidates over the 440 sample photos, all measured in this session: PE-Core-L14-336 and PE-Core-G14-448 zero-shot locally on strixhalo, Shisa DE-2.1 via the hosted Shisa API (development-only comparator by lead decision; the photos went to that API, which is not part of the product pipeline), against the existing Glimmer triage classifications. **There are still zero hand labels, so every number below is inter-system agreement, not accuracy.** Headline: DE-2.1 agrees with Glimmer on category 68.0% of the time, while PE-L and PE-G each agree only ~44% (they agree with each other 67.7%); on speed PE-L is in a different class entirely (0.138 s/photo vs PE-G 1.882, DE-API 2.89 wall at 4 concurrent, Glimmer 19.4). DE abstains far more than Glimmer (unknown on 204/440 vs 94) and its unknown calls cover 96% of Glimmer's unknowns; PE's disagreements concentrate in sightseeing/personal, where the category boundary is definitional. The comparison method (aligned per-photo JSONL from every system, agreement matrix, per-category agreement, confidence means, PE-sim vs DE-positive separation) is reusable as soon as labels or the local DE run land.

## Validation

- Speed (440 photos each): PE-L 60.5 s wall (0.138 s/photo, 3.72 GB peak); PE-G 827.9 s (1.882 s/photo, 13.10 GB peak); DE-API mean 2.89 s/photo wall with 4 concurrent workers for 9 questions/photo (category + 8 evidence yes/no), 0 errors, first-call cold outlier 31 s.
- Category agreement matrix: glimmer↔de-api 68.0%, glimmer↔pe-l 44.3%, glimmer↔pe-g 43.2%, pe-l↔pe-g 67.7%, pe-l↔de-api 43.9%, pe-g↔de-api 39.1%. All four agree on 112/440 (25.5%); at least 3 of 4 on 229/440 (52.0%).
- Per-category agreement vs Glimmer: DE food 87%, unknown 96%, sightseeing 64%; PE-L trip 75% but sightseeing 30% and personal 31%; PE-G personal 59%, sightseeing 34%.
- Evidence signal check (no labels): PE-L cosine sims are higher on DE-positive photos than DE-negative for all 8 evidence labels (dish 0.159 vs 0.061; receipt 0.200 vs 0.097; lodging 0.178 vs 0.097) — the PE evidence signal points the same way as DE's yes/no reads. SAM hit rates from the earlier batch are in the same range as DE positive rates for dish/menu (9.3%/5.9% vs 9.8%/4.8%).
- Confidence means (top category): PE-L 0.686, PE-G 0.681, DE 0.884 (DE image probabilities are raw/uncalibrated per the SDK docs, so cross-system confidence comparison is indicative only).
