# Full-corpus processing status and PRO 6000 rates recorded

## Summary

Full-corpus (9,669-photo) processing status at this checkpoint: import, DINOv3 embeddings (9,669/9,669 after the grayscale fix), and PE-L zero-shot (9,677 rows in 1075 s) are complete; SAM and Glimmer-DE passes are running on the `dev` PRO 6000 box with ETAs of roughly 9 and 7 minutes respectively for their first sweep over the 8,928 already-synced previews, followed by a skip-done tail sweep for the ~736 files the (now crawling) rsync has not delivered. PRO 6000 rates for the shared passes are now tabulated in MODELS.md next to the Strix Halo rates (Glimmer-DE read ~0.19 s vs 2.69 s; SAM ~0.24 s/server vs 2.83 s; generative Glimmer on PRO 6000 still unmeasured).
