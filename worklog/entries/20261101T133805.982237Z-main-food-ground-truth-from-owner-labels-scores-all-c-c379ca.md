# Food ground truth from owner labels scores all classifiers

## Summary

The owner labeled all 440 sample photos in the v2 labeler. By the owner's own account, most non-food photos were confirmed at the model's suggested default, so those labels cannot score the suggesting model fairly (the full-table Glimmer accuracy would be circular). The **61 photos labeled food were individually verified**, so food is the first trustworthy ground-truth slice. On it: Glimmer is strongest (recall 0.984, precision 0.968, F1 0.976 — and this slice is fair even for Glimmer, because the owner scrutinized the food queue rather than rubber-stamping it), DE-2.1 is next on both arms (API F1 0.898; local GGUF F1 0.893 with the best precision, 0.980), and PE zero-shot trails badly on recall (PE-L F1 0.526, PE-G 0.545) because its argmax 'unknown' prompt absorbs most true-food photos (33-36 of the misses). PE's food signal is present but miscalibrated: food is PE-L's top non-unknown category on 45/61 true-food photos, and a 0.1 food-probability threshold lifts its F1 to ~0.64 — still below DE. Remaining bias, stated plainly: food photos that no system called food and the owner confirmed as non-food by default would be invisible in this slice, so all recalls are upper bounds; the non-food categories still have no independent ground truth.

## Changes

- No code changes. Numbers computed from the local labels table (440 latest-pass user labels) joined to the stored per-system results in ignored `data/crumbs/compare/*.jsonl` and the DB's Glimmer classifications; comparison script run from `/tmp`.
- `MODELS.md`: PE and DE rows gain the food-slice F1 figures with their scope note.

## Validation

- Label distribution (440): trip 121, sightseeing 104, other 94, food 61, personal 60 — matching the model distributions outside food confirms the owner's 'mostly default' account, which is why only food is scored as ground truth.
- Food slice (n=61): Glimmer recall 60/61, precision 60/62; DE-API 53/61 recall, 0.930 precision; DE-local 50/61 recall, 0.980 precision (one false positive); PE-L 25/61 and PE-G 24/61 recall, with unknowns as the dominant miss.
- PE-L threshold sweep on the same slice: food-probability thresholds 0.1/0.2/0.3/0.5 give recall 0.656/0.590/0.492/0.410 against 24/16/12/6 false positives in 379 non-food photos.

## Next

- If independent ground truth for the other categories is wanted, the efficient route is scoring disagreements: have the owner adjudicate only the photos where DE and Glimmer (or PE) disagree, rather than re-labeling everything.
- PE food routing, if used, should threshold the food probability (or drop the unknown prompt for this decision) instead of taking the 5-way argmax.
