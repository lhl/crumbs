# DE-2.1 np1 re-measured on idle gfx1151; np4 arm needs an owned restart

## Summary

The DE comparator host `gfx1151` is reachable again on the tailnet over a direct path and is idle. Exactly one DE-2.1 `llama-server` is running there, and its flags are identical to the launch recorded in `20261101T111230.863209Z-main-local-de-2-1-gguf-on-gfx1151-box-completes-class-3dc1f4.md`, so ownership of that process and its model assets is established rather than assumed. Nothing else GPU-bearing was running, and nothing was started, stopped, or reconfigured.

The np1 arm of the interrupted parallelism benchmark was re-measured on the same 12 photos with the same runner: **0.35-0.37 s/photo at 1, 4 and 8 client workers** (mean 0.363 over six runs, 4.40 s wall per 12 photos), plus **0.80 s/photo** on the first batch after roughly eleven hours idle. Client concurrency changes wall time by under 1%, which is the expected shape for a single server slot that serializes every request: np1 throughput is slot-bound, not client-bound. The `1.40 s/photo at 1 worker` and `0.83 s/photo at 4 workers` figures carried in from the interrupted session did **not** reproduce on the now-idle box; they are recorded here as historical only.

The np4 arm was not run. `-np` is a launch-time flag, this build exposes no runtime slot reconfiguration, and the coordinator reserved persistent server launches for the parent, so the required restart was left undone instead of half-done. That is the concrete blocker, and the exact stop/launch/bench/restore sequence is in **Next**. The state found on the host is unchanged and still serving.
