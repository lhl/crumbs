# Frame impact and technical claims around local agentic product

## Summary

Recorded the lead's product framing in BRAINSTORMING.md. Impact claim is deliberately honest: Crumbs saves no lives; its case is qualitative usefulness at frequent, ordinary moments (recommending, reordering, arriving, planning) for anyone whose photos cannot answer questions — and it only holds if answers are fast, correct, and evidence-backed, which is why abstention and corrections are core. Two agentic product behaviors are now explicit: a background, always-on local agent that organizes new photos into visits as they arrive and interrupts only with targeted questions, and contextual surfacing (near a past place, at a repeat restaurant, mid-conversation or trip planning) that is acceptable precisely because triggers and history stay on-device, are user-configured, and link to evidence — with precision over recall as the stated bar. Technical-depth claim is likewise made explicit: a fully local multi-model stack we serve, post-train, calibrate, and evaluate ourselves, not an API wrapper.

## Changes

- `BRAINSTORMING.md`: new sections “The background agent: organized without being asked” and “Contextual options: the right memory at the right moment”; Impact bullet rewritten with the honest qualitative/frequency-based claim and its correctness precondition; Technical depth bullet now states the local multi-model, self-served/post-trained/evaluated stack; Demo bullet gains a contextual-surfacing moment.

## Validation

- Documentation-only framing change; consistency re-checked against the local-only decision in DISCOVERY.md and the rubric text in README.md (Impact 30%, Technical Depth & Execution 20%). No capability claims added beyond what DISCOVERY's experiments are designed to measure.
