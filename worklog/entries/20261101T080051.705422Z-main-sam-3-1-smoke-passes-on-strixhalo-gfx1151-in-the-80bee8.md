# SAM 3.1 smoke passes on strixhalo gfx1151 in therock env

## Summary

SAM 3.1 now runs on strixhalo (the second Strix Halo box) as well as zbook, so segmentation work for EXT-04 can proceed without interrupting Glimmer serving on zbook. The bf16 checkpoint was copied from zbook and md5-verified; in the `therock` mamba env (torch 2.14.0+rocm10.1.0, transformers 5.8.1) on gfx1151 it loads in 8.2 s, peaks at 2.30 GB torch-allocated unified memory (3.19 GB process RSS), and answers text prompts at ~0.5 s each after a warmup first prompt — matching the zbook rates in MODELS.md. A `sign` mask on a corpus photo (sightseeing, trail-guide signboard in Pingxi) scored 0.848 at 66.3% coverage and was visually confirmed to be exactly the signboard.
