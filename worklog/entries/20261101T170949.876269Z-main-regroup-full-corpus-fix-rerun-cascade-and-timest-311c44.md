# Regroup full corpus: fix rerun cascade and timestamp mixing

## Summary

Regrouping the full 9,669-photo corpus surfaced three scale bugs in the deterministic stages, all fixed: (1) `group` could not rerun at all — its regeneration deletes only its own child rows, but `visit_place_candidates`, `visit_stays`, `visit_resolutions`, and `review_questions` also reference visits and blocked deletion with a foreign-key error (they are all derived and regenerated by later stages, so the cascade now clears them for the algorithm's own visits); (2) five photos carry empty-string timestamps from EXIF, which crashed timestamp parsing in both `group` and the timezone estimator — empty strings are now treated as untimed; (3) the 2025/2026 corpus mixes offset-aware ISO timestamps (newer iPhone EXIF) with naive ones, and naive/aware subtraction crashed grouping — timestamps are normalized to camera-local wall time in `group` (its documented semantics) and to aware UTC in `locations`. Results on the full corpus: 590 visits and 20 trips (562 untimed photos reported, not dropped); timezone estimates for 8,200 GPS photos with 7,501 within 1 km of the interpolated track at the best offset; 554 stays; 56 of 546 GPS-centered visits matched to a stay. Note the tz offset histogram piles up at the +14 search edge for 1,349 photos — tz-v1's whole-hour track matching is coarse there and those estimates should not be over-trusted.

## Changes

- `crumbs/group.py`: downstream-child cleanup before visit deletion; empty-string timestamps excluded as untimed; `_naive()` camera-local normalization applied at all three parse sites.
- `crumbs/locations.py`: `_epoch_ms()` helper (aware strings keep their instant, naive keep face-value UTC); empty-string filters in both photo queries; correlate window parsing normalized to aware UTC.

## Validation

- `crumbs group` completes: {'visits': 590, 'trips': 20, 'untimed_photos': 562, 'visit_size_median': 3.0, 'visit_size_max': 356, 'single_photo_visits': 203}.
- `crumbs tz`: 8,200 photos, 7,501 within 1 km at best offset. `crumbs stays`: 554 kept (unchanged inputs). `crumbs correlate`: 56/546 visits matched, 59 links; photo/track agreement 7,501 of 8,166 with a track position.
- `python3 -m py_compile crumbs/group.py crumbs/locations.py` passes.
