# Import Wanderings history, derive stays, cross-correlate photos

## Summary

DAT-02/IMP-02 landed for the Wanderings export and it is the strongest corroboration source we have. Imported 25,165 raw location points (GeoJSON, epoch-ms UTC, accuracy kept, `observed`) spanning 2018–2026 (4,610 in the 2019 corpus year; 19,132 points pass the ≤200 m accuracy filter). Derived 554 dwell stays (≥20 min within 150 m). The headline result is IMP-03 falling out of the data: photo EXIF times are camera-local while the track is UTC, so face-value agreement was only 142/399 photos within 1 km — but estimating each photo's whole-hour UTC offset by matching its GPS against the interpolated track puts **374/399 (94%) within 1 km**, with offsets clustering on real zones (home −7/−6, Taiwan +8, Japan +9). Stored per-photo in `tz_estimates`. With corrected clocks: 20 of 218 visits match a dwell stay (closest center distance 2.9 m — the matched set is essentially certain), 535 stays have no *sampled-photo* visit (mostly out-of-scope years/home dwells, not yet 'dark visits' until scope-filtered), and a broader track-presence test shows 52/58 food visits (90%) have the track within 1 km during the visit (36 within 250 m) while only 3 coincide with a derived dwell stay. How to best use this info, recorded as guidance: (1) **track presence/dwell is visit corroboration** — an independent witness that upgrades a photo cluster from inferred toward confirmed-grade evidence, and the dwell center is a better position than sampled photo GPS for ranking place candidates; (2) **stays without photos** are the recommendation-completeness set (places attended but not photographed) once scope/year-filtered; (3) **the track is the timezone oracle** for IMP-03 and for converting the whole corpus to true UTC; (4) overlaps of *dwell* stays with restaurant candidates (dwell ≥20 min within ~150 m of a candidate) are the strongest non-check-in triangulation signal short of signage text.

## Changes

- `crumbs/db.py`: `location_records`, `stays`, `visit_stays`, `tz_estimates` tables.
- `crumbs/locations.py`: Wanderings GeoJSON importer (idempotent-shaped via source_imports), greedy dwell-stay derivation (accuracy-filtered, running-center clusters), track interpolation, per-photo UTC-offset estimation (`tz-v1`), and tz-corrected visit/stay correlation.
- `crumbs/cli.py`: `import-locations`, `stays`, `tz`, `correlate` subcommands. All personal data stays in the ignored `data/` store.

## Validation

- `import-locations`: 25,165 points. `stays`: 554 kept from 17,839 raw clusters (19,132 points used). `tz`: 374/399 within 1 km at best offset; histogram peaks at −7 (76), +9 (58), −6 (38), +8 (28), +10 (27) with a spurious-looking +14 tail (37) flagged below.
- `correlate` after tz correction: 20 visits matched to stays (links 23), photo agreement 374/399 within 1 km. Food-visit track-presence analysis (computed from the stored track): 36/58 within 250 m, 16 more within 1 km, 6 beyond.
- Known imperfections recorded, not smoothed over: stay-v1 clustering fragments under accuracy jitter (17.8k raw clusters is too many; a fixed-anchor or two-pass merge is the v2 fix), and the +14-offset photo group needs inspection before tz values are trusted per-photo (hourly granularity + sparse track can alias); visit-level medians are the robust unit, which is what correlate() uses.
