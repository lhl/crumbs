# Visit reconciliation with evidence scoring (REC-02 v1)

## Summary

REC-02 v1 reconciles all 228 visits by deterministic evidence scoring (PLAN triangulation order): signage-text match +50, dwell-stay proximity +25/+15, track presence within 250 m +10, GPS proximity 0–20, food evidence +5. Outcome: 2 candidate-strong (exactly the two signage-matched visits — the scoring cannot manufacture confidence GPS alone), 29 candidate-weak, 197 unknown, and 56 single targeted review questions generated for food visits that stayed unresolved. Nothing is confirmed by this stage, by construction; confirmation remains check-in/signage-accepted/user territory (REC-03). Every resolution stores its ranked reasons, so any individual result can be audited back to evidence in one query. The honest read of the distribution: most photo visits are not food visits with known nearby places, so 'unknown' dominating is the correct answer for a system that must not guess restaurants.

## Changes

- `crumbs/db.py`: `visit_resolutions` (status/tier/reasons, per-algorithm unique) and `review_questions` (one open question per unresolved food visit) tables.
- `crumbs/reconcile.py`: evidence scoring, tz-corrected track-presence test, margin rule for candidate-strong (>=45 and >=10 over runner-up), question generation. `crumbs/cli.py`: `reconcile` subcommand.

## Validation

- `reconcile: {'visits': 228, 'candidate-strong': 2, 'candidate-weak': 29, 'unknown': 197, 'questions': 56}`; the two strong rows inspected — both are the signage-text matches with food+GPS support and itemized reasons.
- Sample review questions read naturally and cite the leading candidate with its distance evidence.
