# Selective extraction pass v1 with signage matching (EXT)

## Summary

EXT v1 ran over the evidence-bearing slice (69 targets = food-category ∪ triage-evidence photos): 67 extracted in 34 min (≈30 s/photo, the extraction row now in MODELS.md), 2 failed and remain resumable (one malformed JSON from the model, one 1,100-token truncation on a text-dense trail sign). Yield: 38/67 with visible-text transcription, 31 with dish candidates (Japanese dish names read from menus/signage, e.g. steak-lunch and donburi items), 17 with place-name candidates, 15 honest `cannot_determine`. Signage-text promotion produced only **2** new PlaceCandidates (both plausible: a Seattle restaurant at 103 m, a Japanese seafood chain at 89 m) — conservative by construction (name similarity ≥0.75 AND a registry place within 500 m), and limited by real mismatches: extracted names are often street names, brands, or romanizations that the OSM registry near food visits does not carry in the same form. That is the honest E3/E4-relevant signal: transcription and dish extraction are already useful; *name matching* needs either script-aware normalization (Japanese/romaji) or check-in/user confirmation to promote at scale — it is not a silent-failure to paper over. Note for operators, learned here: syncing the SQLite DB while its WAL is hot corrupts the copy; checkpoint (`PRAGMA wal_checkpoint(TRUNCATE)`) before any rsync of `crumbs.db`.
