# Deterministic visit/trip grouping (REC-01 v1)

## Summary

REC-01 v1 groups the corpus deterministically (no model): sort by EXIF time; split visits on >6 h gaps or >50 km GPS jumps; split trips on >=4-day gaps between visits. Result on the 440-photo sample: 228 candidate visits and 14 trips; the largest visits are coherent (single-afternoon clusters at consistent coordinates, both home-area and Japan travel clusters resolve correctly). Two honest limitations, both properties of the *sampled* corpus rather than the algorithm: 158 of 228 visits are single-photo (systematic 40/month sampling thins bursts that would merge in the full corpus), and trip segmentation over-merges at this sparsity (two 'trips' span 6–10 weeks because sampled gaps rarely exceed 4 days). Conclusion: grouping parameters must be validated on the full corpus import, not this sample — the sample proves mechanics and plumbing. 27 photos lack EXIF time and are excluded with a count, not silently dropped. Times remain camera-local until IMP-03.
