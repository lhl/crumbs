# Sweep other open Meta models into DISCOVERY

## Summary

Swept Meta's open-model releases beyond the six-line hackathon catalog and folded the relevant ones into DISCOVERY.md's test plan. Biggest correction: SAM 3.1 is not API-only — Meta released open SAM 3 (Nov 2025) and SAM 3.1 (Mar 2026) checkpoints (848M, CUDA/PyTorch, SAM License, gated Hugging Face access), so local segmentation returns to the plan as E4 and the earlier API-only exclusion in DISCOVERY/BRAINSTORMING is corrected. Other additions: Perception Encoder (Apache 2.0 CLIP-style image/text embeddings and zero-shot classification) as DINOv3's challenger in E1, Perception Language Model 1B/3B/8B (FAIR Research License, strong published document/TextVQA numbers) as a smaller local extraction comparator in E3, Meta CLIP 2 and SeamlessM4T/MMS as conditional probes (the latter could remove the voice cloud exception; CC BY-NC 4.0), Llama 4 Scout as a last-resort local VLM comparator, and a ruled-out/watchlist record (V-JEPA 2, ImageBind, Nougat, SAM 2 fallback, PE-AV/SAM Audio, older FAIR stacks). All remain supporting models: none counts toward the hackathon's named Glimmer/Spark Open bonus.
