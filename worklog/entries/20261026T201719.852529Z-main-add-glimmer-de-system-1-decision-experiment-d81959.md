# Add Glimmer-DE System-1 decision experiment

## Summary

Added DISCOVERY experiment E2b: turn Muse Glimmer into a System-1 decision model (working name Glimmer-DE) following the lead's Shisa DE-2.1 pattern — LoRA training with cross-entropy over letter-coded option tokens at the answer boundary, a single-forward restricted-logprob read (max_tokens=1, no generated text), per-read temperature calibration fitted on our data, and a think-when-unsure escalation to generative Glimmer. Its Crumbs job is the high-volume routing layer: category and evidence-type decisions, evidence-sufficiency and visit-boundary calls, and abstention, with calibrated probabilities driving thresholds. The known cost is stated plainly: Glimmer is a 30B dense model, so each read is far slower than DE-2.1's 3.8B-active MoE (37 ms p50 single read on one RTX PRO 6000 per its card); E2b measures per-read and per-photo-set latency under vLLM batching, against PE zero-shot, the DINOv3 head, and DE-2.1 itself as pattern reference, with two scoped fallbacks (decision layer only for embedding-hard calls, or distillation into a PLM-3B DE student).

## Changes

- `DISCOVERY.md`: new E2b (feasibility checks: single-token option codes in Glimmer's tokenizer, restricted-logprob readout coverage in vLLM/SGLang, completion-only LoRA objective; training data rendered from E0 labels with shuffled option order and an explicit none/unknown option; multi-seed soup at reduced scale; metrics for accuracy, ECE/calibration, and per-photo question-set latency; pass/kill/scope rules); sources added for Glimmer post-training and the DE-2.1 model card.
- `BRAINSTORMING.md`: Glimmer candidate role now includes the Glimmer-DE System-1 variant.

## Validation

- DE-2.1 facts verified against its public model card (fetched 2026-10-27): Gemma 4 26B A4B base, eleven rank-16 LoRA runs souped, vision encoder untrained, readout contract and calibration tables, PRO 6000 latency. Glimmer training feasibility verified against Meta's post-training docs (Axolotl/TRL LoRA/QLoRA, vision tower frozen in adapter configs, vision QLoRA example). No Glimmer-DE training or serving has been run; all performance questions are explicitly E2b measurements.
